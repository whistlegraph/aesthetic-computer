Usage: ac [directory] [--runtime mjs|lisp|processing]
                      [--piece FILE] [--resume THREAD_ID]
                      [--genre piece|nopaint]
                      [--backend claude|codex|ac|open] [--model NAME]
                      [--autopublish | --no-autopublish]
                      [--pro] [--private]
       aesthetic [directory]
       aesthetic doctor
       aesthetic login | logout | whoami
       aesthetic publish <file> [slug]
       aesthetic colors <colour…>
       aesthetic mime <file> ["caption"]

Open the aesel terminal interface in a workspace, or manage the
shared Aesthetic Computer sign-in (~/.ac-token) and publish a piece under
your @handle at https://aesthetic.computer/@handle/slug.

Each session starts by choosing an AC blank piece or a nopaint.art brush,
then shows a QR code that runs it live on a phone. --genre makes that
choice at launch; --runtime picks an AC piece's language.

--backend picks the engine bridge. claude (the default, on claude-opus-5)
and codex each drive that vendor's CLI and need a subscription to it. ac
needs neither -- it runs on Aesthetic Computer's own account, metered
against your @handle. --model names the model on that bridge, and
/backend and /model change either one mid-session.

--autopublish publishes the piece under your @handle a couple of seconds
after every save, so the session's URL is live the whole time it is being
worked on. On by default, because the address on the prompt rock is that
published URL and it has to answer from the first second.
--no-autopublish or AESEL_AUTOPUBLISH=0 turns it off, and /autopublish
toggles it mid-session.

Pro is the default here (`piece` or --piece/--genre asks for the piece
interface instead). Pro is the same interface as a general harness: no piece, no QR, nothing
published, and the engine runs with your own settings, skills and MCP
servers (approvals still come back here; /ask off runs without asking).
--private keeps this session's subject out of the Slab marker and the
transcript index. Either can also be set per directory in
~/.config/aesel/profiles.json (/profile shows the shape).
