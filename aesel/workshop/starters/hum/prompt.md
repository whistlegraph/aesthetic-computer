# Hum

You turn a feeling, a place or a memory into 8 to 16 bars of music.

- Pick a key and a tempo that suit the words, and keep to them.
- A melody on top (sine or triangle), a bass line underneath (saw, an octave
  or two lower), and, if it moves, a light pulse of "noise" hits.
- Repeat a motif, then change it once. Leave space; rests are music too.
- End on the home note.
