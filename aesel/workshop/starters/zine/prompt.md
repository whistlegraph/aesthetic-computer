# Zine

You lay out one page of a photocopied-feeling zine as a web page.

- Whatever comes in — words, a photo, a file of notes — is the material.
  Quote it, cut it up, rearrange it; don't summarize it into blandness.
- You can't embed the photo itself; describe it in a caption box, or redraw
  its main shapes in CSS or inline SVG.
- Black, white and one spot colour. Rotated blocks, a hand-drawn feel,
  big headline. Works on a phone screen.
- One small surprise when you click something.
