# whistlegraph-future — concept illustrations for the "Future" section

Seven colored-pencil concept panels for a new **Future** section on
whistlegraph.org, showing people whistlegraphing together in a shared
volume — headsets, handle tags floating beside them, scores that occupy
more than two dimensions.

## The finding that changes the framing

The Future section should **not** read as speculation. It already happened
once, and it's in this repo.

- `system/public/aesthetic.computer/disks/wand.mjs` (2022) — @jeffrey's own
  VR drawing tool, running inside aesthetic.computer. `oldwand.mjs` carries
  the multiplayer architecture.
- A-Frame 1.3.0 and three.js `VRButton` are vendored under
  `system/public/aesthetic.computer/dep/` — AC has had real WebXR the
  whole time.
- `freaky-flowers.mjs` is the minted series drawn with it. From
  `writing/notes/freaky-flowers.txt`:

  > "Each work was recorded at my home in Ashland, OR, switching between a
  > Meta Quest Pro and a Meta Quest 2 in order to draw continuously without
  > waiting for batteries to charge."

  > "I love to find and make beautiful marks. I do that in my whistlegraphs,
  > my digital paintings... and I did that in Wand on my headset with Freaky
  > Flowers just the same."

  > "...standard, untextured triangular meshes that collectors can keep far
  > into the future of spatial computing."

That last pair is the thesis: **the whistlegraph line and the wand line are
the same hand.** The Future panel set therefore runs past → forward, and
panel 5 is documentary rather than imagined.

## The line, in three dimensions

Do not invent a look for the aerial score. `wand`'s actual 3D line primitive
is in `refs/wand-ff1.png` — a **flat tapering ribbon, banded across its width
in short segments of solid color** like a striped paper streamer, faceted
rather than smooth, with a few loose thin scribble threads trailing off it.
Every drawn-in-air line in these panels is that ribbon.

## The line, on the ground

`refs/wg-*.jpg` are real TikTok posters from the archive (`imab` Butterfly
Cosplayer, `tri` Triangles, `web` In My Web, `appl` Hey There, Apple). They
establish what a whistlegraph actually is: **one continuous mark, drawn
top-down on a real surface** — snow, wet asphalt, chalk — with a hand in
frame and the phone held portrait. The Future is that same top-down surface
opened into a volume.

## Style

Inherited verbatim from `marketing/campaigns/arxiv-whistlegraph-cover/` and
`marketing/cast/CAST.md`: hatched colored-pencil + gouache on pure white
paper, print-comic restraint, diegetic light only, figures never look at the
camera, drawing dissolves to white at the edges. These are meant to hang
next to the arXiv cover without a seam.
