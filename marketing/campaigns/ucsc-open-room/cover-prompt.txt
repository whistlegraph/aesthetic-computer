A photograph of a colored-pencil and gouache drawing on cream paper, landscape. Confident hatching and striping build tone. Tapered pencil edges. Visible paper grain. Print-work sensibility. Diegetic light only. Candid, deadpan — NOT cinematic, NOT an advertisement. Nobody looks at the camera. No caption, no wordmark, no lettering anywhere on the page. NO motion blur, NO soft-focus haze — crisp pencil from foreground to back.

the picture is one sheet divided into six equal panels, three across and two down, thin pencil gutters between them. six different rooms in six different places, one person in each, all the same size, all at the same eye level — a class that meets online, drawn as the rooms it actually happens in.

top left: the man from the reference photos at a small kitchen table, morning light from a window at his left, a chartreuse yellow-green laptop open in front of him with the lid toward us so the screen is hidden — over the lid center a small torn white-paper scrap with a thick hand-penned butterfly in dark marker, two big rounded wings. his eyes are on his own screen. plain relaxed button-down, top button undone, a bright-yellow torpedo-shaped fountain pen with a gold clip in the chest pocket.

top middle: a woman in her fifties on a porch swing, phone held up in both hands, back of the phone to us, headphones on.
top right: a young man on a city bus, phone in one hand, the window behind him full of hatched buildings.
bottom left: a teenager cross-legged on a bunk bed, a scuffed black laptop on their knees, lid toward us.
bottom middle: a woman in a hijab at a laundromat folding table, a small laptop open, lid toward us, a basket of clothes beside it.
bottom right: an older man at a garage workbench, a phone propped against a coffee can, a soldering iron resting nearby, looking at the phone.

every laptop lid faces the viewer with no screen visible; every phone shows its back. no two rooms share a color — each room has its own light: warm window, cool porch shade, bus fluorescent, bedside lamp, laundromat white, bare garage bulb. warm and ordinary. the six panels together read as one room.
