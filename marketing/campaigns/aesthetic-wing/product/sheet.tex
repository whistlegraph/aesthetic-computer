\vspace{0.45em}
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.54\linewidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\head{wingmint}{The short version}
Aesthetic Wing is an electric one-wheel that can make an \textbf{air step}.
Most of the time it rolls. When the surface changes, two guarded lift petals
open, progressively unload the wheel, rise through a bounded envelope, and
settle back onto the street.

It is not a flying car. It proposes a smaller gesture between riding and flying:
\textbf{a little more street, in every direction.}

\head{wingcoral}{The research question}
Can a compact one-wheel vehicle transition safely between rolling, partial lift,
and controlled low hover while preserving an intuitive board-riding experience?

\head{wingblue}{The interface}
\tag{lean to move}\quad\tag{press to rise}\quad\tag{relax to settle}

The rider expresses intent through stance and foot pressure. Stabilization,
height limiting, energy reserve, and automatic settling remain machine duties.

\head{wingsun}{Truth before spectacle}
The illustration is a product vision, not flight-test evidence. Phase I contains
no rider, no public-street operation, and no unrestricted altitude.
\end{minipage}\hfill

\titleblock{wingblue}{Phase I research program}

\head{wingblue}{Operating modes}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{>{\bfseries}p{1.1in}X}
Roll & One central wheel supports ordinary efficient travel.\\
Skim & Lift partially unloads the wheel over rough ground.\\
Air step & A short controlled rise, translation, and landing.\\
Hover & Fully airborne movement inside a deliberately low envelope.\\
Safe settle & Automatic descent after lost input, low reserve, or fault.\\
\end{tabularx}

\vspace{0.4em}
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.49\linewidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\head{wingmint}{Phase I objectives}
\begin{enumerate}
  \setlength{\itemsep}{0.22em}
  \item Model mass, lift area, power, thermal behavior, downwash, noise, and transition dynamics.
  \item Validate guarded propulsion on a stationary full-load test article.
  \item Develop roll-to-hover controls and automatic settling in simulation and hardware.
  \item Demonstrate an unmanned weighted prototype rolling, unloading, crossing a controlled interruption, landing, and rolling again.
\end{enumerate}

\head{wingcoral}{Safety gates}
Rotor containment; independent shutdown; tethered initial trials; battery
isolation; thermal monitoring; motor-out testing; bounded height and speed;
formal hazard review before every expansion of the envelope.
\end{minipage}\hfill

\head{wingmint}{Funding request}
\textbf{\$300,000 over 12 months} to establish technical feasibility and
demonstrate an unmanned wheel-to-hover transition. The non-confidential funding
thesis is a deployable distributed-lift and transition-control system for
bounded personal mobility. The product and cultural identity remain
\textbf{Aesthetic Wing}.
