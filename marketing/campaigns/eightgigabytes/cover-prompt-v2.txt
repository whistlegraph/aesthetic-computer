Square single cover for "eight gigabytes", a small electronic pop song sung by three laptops about themselves.

Subject: three MacBook Neo laptops, drawn exactly from the reference photos (their proportions, thin bezels, rounded corners, keyboard and trackpad), standing open side by side on a plain warm off-white table, FACING THE VIEWER head-on so we see their screens and keyboards. Their bodies are in the three reference colors: citrus (yellow-green) in the middle and slightly forward as the lead singer, indigo (blue) on the right, blush (pink) on the left.

Style: crisp colored-pencil illustration with confident lines and visible paper grain for the laptops, table and shadows. Centered, one focal arrangement, generous empty space above.
