Square single cover for "eight gigabytes", a small electronic pop song sung by three laptops about themselves.

Subject: three MacBook Neo laptops, drawn exactly from the reference photos (their proportions, thin bezels, rounded corners, keyboard and trackpad), standing open close together on a plain warm off-white table, FACING THE VIEWER head-on so we see their screens and keyboards. Their bodies are in the three reference colors: citrus (yellow-green) in the middle and slightly forward as the lead singer, indigo (blue) on the right, blush (pink) on the left.

Each screen fills edge to edge with that laptop's own cartoon face, copied faithfully from the three face reference images: the GREEN face (green field, spiky tuft, raised brows, big round eyes, wide open singing mouth) on the citrus laptop; the BLUE face (blue field, row of black bumps for hair, heavy-lidded eyes, rosy cheeks, open singing mouth) on the indigo laptop; the PINK face (pink field, curly hairs, round eyes, freckles, open singing mouth) on the blush laptop. Style: crisp colored-pencil illustration with confident lines and visible paper grain for the laptops, table and shadows.
