A warm, hand-drawn illustration for a music-piece poster — painterly and poster-like, not a
photo. Soft off-white paper ground, confident inked outlines, gentle gouache-like shading, a
little hand-drawn texture and grain, subtle drop shadows.

Scene: the young man from the portrait reference — shoulder-length light-brown hair, kind

bare wooden floor in the CENTRE of the frame, seen from a three-quarter angle slightly ABOVE

like a player listening between phrases.

Around him, THREE small thin laptops stand open on the floor in a wide ring, one to his
left, one behind and above him, one to his right, each turned inward to face the circle.

doubled. Each machine's base sits flat on the floorboards with an honest contact shadow
beneath it.
