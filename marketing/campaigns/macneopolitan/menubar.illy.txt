A warm, hand-drawn illustration — painterly and poster-like, not a photo, not a screenshot.
Soft off-white paper ground, confident inked outlines, gentle gouache-like shading, a little
hand-drawn texture and grain.

Scene: an extreme close-up of the TOP-RIGHT CORNER of one open laptop screen, drawn large
and centred so the strip of interface runs across the middle of the frame. We are looking
straight on at the top edge of the display. The rest of the screen below is a soft plain
wash of colour with nothing on it; the machine's bezel and the thin top edge of its lid
frame the strip.

The machine is the BLUSH MacBook Neo. Its aluminium is a soft dusty pink, warm, pale and
clearly desaturated — around #dfc7c7, a barely-there pink. It must NOT be a saturated rose,
magenta or bubblegum pink. Match the Apple product photograph supplied as a reference.

Running along that top edge, left to right, draw a row of small simple menu-bar symbols,
evenly spaced, all at the same size and on the same baseline:
• a small WiFi arc symbol
• a small battery symbol, drawn as a rounded rectangle about two-thirds full
• then, sitting in the row exactly like the others, A TINY PIANO — a small horizontal strip
  of miniature keyboard, pale near-white natural keys with a few narrow deeper accent keys
  standing among them, three of its keys lit and gently glowing in deep teal
• then the time, drawn as an unreadable soft suggestion of small tick-marks and shapes, NOT
  as legible words or numerals

The tiny piano is the same visual weight and size as the WiFi and battery beside it — it
belongs in that row, an ordinary built-in facility of the machine, not a special decoration.
That equality is the whole point of the picture.

Three or four small musical notes rise gently from the lit keys of the tiny piano and drift
up past the top edge of the frame, tinted deep teal, sparse and delicate.

Keep it clean, quiet and elegant, with generous negative space. No text, no words, no
readable numerals, no letters, no logos, no brand marks, no fruit symbol, no hands, no
people, no cursor, no open windows or popovers.
