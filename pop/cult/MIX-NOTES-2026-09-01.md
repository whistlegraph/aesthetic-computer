## 1. The opening is too extreme — the phone sounds

> "its nice but the opening i think is too extreme … with all the phone sounds
> … i wish we could get it to be more subtle / better mixed in"

The final's first seconds are two score fragments, not one:

- **0:00–0:04** = source 15.95–20.0 s = **bars 8–10** — `phone-pickup-a`
  (`bus: "vox"`, gain 0.20) plus the first of the three voices.
- **0:04 onward** = source 58 s = **bar 29**, the two bars of phone harmonics
  that set up the first "run real fast" at bar 31.

So the listener meets the record through the phone layer twice over, back to
back, with the edit removing everything (bars 10–29) that would have made it a
gradual arrival. Candidates: the bar-8 pickup gain, the bar-29/30 phone
harmonics, and the SIGNAL bus half-depth duck that is supposed to seat the
phone under the bed.

## 2. Bass and guitar attack/decay around 1:12 — "marching band heavy"

> "the attack / decay of the bass notes especilly / guitar is a little marching
> band heavy / weird around 1:12"

1:12 maps to source ~133.95 s = **bar ~67**, early in `spread: [64, 76]`.
What is playing there:

- `guitar-chug` — the palm-muted B-pedal, `gb = 64` with `dur: 7.45`, so one
  held chug covers bars 64–67.7 (render10.mjs:1802–1810).
- `guitarChord` — square block chords every 4 bars on the Bm/D/G/Em cycle,
  `dur: 3.25`, `gb = 64` (render10.mjs:1826).

A pedal chug plus downbeat block chords with no inner motion is exactly the
"marching band" read.

## 3. The guitar there should be more flowery / jazzy

> "i wish the guyitar there was more flowery / jazzzy"

Same place as note 2. The vocabulary already exists in the lane —
`guitarShred()` (render10.mjs:485) does bowed runs, and `guitarChord()` takes
an `up` flag for rake direction — but bar 64–68 uses neither. Nothing voices
the chords beyond root-position four-note stacks (the `GC` table,
render10.mjs:1818–1823): no extensions, no passing tones.

## 4. Attenuative dip before the spatial explosions

> "before the explosions / spatial explositions there is liek an attenuative
> dip … id prefer it was more smooth transition thewre"

The `blast` explosion is `bar: 76` → final **1:22** (this is the "bus-dispersed
1:23 blast"). Suspected mechanism, **not yet confirmed by measurement**:
`elasticizeBus()` (render10.mjs:2633+) attenuates by
`distance = 1 / (1 + 0.24 * travel)` and never boosts, so the whole bus can
only get quieter as the body travels; combined with `attack`/`release` edges
and `phraseLevel` breathing into the section change, the approach reads as a
duck instead of a lift.
