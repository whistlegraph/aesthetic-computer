a photograph of a colored-pencil and gouache drawing on deep indigo paper.
square, 1:1. seen from directly overhead, straight down, flat plan view.

a wide dark dancefloor with only seven people on it, spaced far apart, and one
of them is beginning to come uncurled.

each person is the same figure from the reference score, drawn as one
continuous white chalk line: sitting curled up, knees to the chest, arms
wrapped round the shins, head down on the knees, long thin lines trailing past
the feet. wobbling, un-erased, one unbroken stroke each, overlaps visible.
six of them hold that posture exactly. the seventh, slightly left of centre,
has lifted its head and let one arm fall open — the same single line, the same
hand, just opened. it is the only change in the picture.

they sit on the score's grid but most of the grid is empty: the seven are
scattered across six rows of five, with whole rows and columns left bare. the
emptiness is the subject. no two figures are near each other.

light is diegetic and comes from the floor: one small coloured lamp set into
the boards beside each figure — magenta, cyan, chartreuse, amber, violet —
each throwing a pool that reaches to the edge of its own person and stops.
seven little islands of light in a very large dark floor. the boards are
visible in the pools: plain worn wood, no pattern, no logo.
