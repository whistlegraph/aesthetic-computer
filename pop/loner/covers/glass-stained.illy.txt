amber. the glass is not flat colour — it is streaky and full of bubbles and
striations, darker where it is thicker, and it glows where the light comes
through from behind.

around the figure the panel is glazed in near-black glass so the coloured
pieces read as the only light in the picture. the panel fills the frame.
