a brush-and-ink drawing on warm off-white paper, photographed flat. square, 1:1.

one figure alone: a person sitting curled up, knees drawn to the chest, both
arms wrapped around the shins, head tipped forward and resting on the knees,
long lines trailing down past the feet and running off the bottom of the paper.

drawn with a loaded round brush in one weight of black ink — thick, wet,
confident strokes that swell and thin, visible bristle drag at the ends, a
couple of places where the line doubles back over itself and pools darker. the
hand is quick and unfussy; nothing is corrected, nothing is filled in. the
figure is a contour only, no shading, no hatching, no interior detail.

the figure is large and fills most of the sheet, sitting slightly below centre
with clean empty paper around it. the paper has a faint tooth and a warm tone.

no words anywhere in the picture.

NO photorealism, NO 3d rendering, NO chrome or metal, NO gradients, NO glow, NO
colour other than black ink and paper, NO title lettering, NO artist name, NO
logo, NO barcode, NO signature, NO lettering of any kind.
