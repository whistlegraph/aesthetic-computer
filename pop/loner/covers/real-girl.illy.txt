a photograph. square, 1:1. one young woman, alone, sitting on the floor of an
empty nightclub after everyone has gone.

she is in her twenties. she sits curled up exactly as in the reference drawing
— knees drawn up to her chest, both arms wrapped around her shins, hands
clasped at the front, her head tipped forward and resting down toward her
knees so her face is mostly hidden. her back is rounded, her weight is on her
hip and one forearm rests along her shin. the pose is the drawing's pose,
carried over one-to-one.

she is dressed plainly and warmly for a club at four in the morning: an
oversized dark tee or a plain long-sleeve, loose trousers, socks, one shoe
kicked off beside her. long hair falling forward past her face and down over
her arms, and it keeps falling — the hair and a few loose threads trail down
onto the floor and run away from her along the boards, the way the long lines
trail off the bottom of the drawing. she is composed and self-contained, not
distressed, not posed for anyone.

she is large in the frame — she fills most of it, seen from slightly above and
in front, close. the floor around her is dark worn wood, almost black.

the light is the record's: a deep indigo falling from above and behind her,
and one hot magenta source low and to the left throwing colour across the
floor and up the underside of her arms and jaw. the background deepens from
near-black indigo at the top to a saturated magenta at the bottom edge. warm
skin against cold dark. high contrast, rich colour, no haze.
