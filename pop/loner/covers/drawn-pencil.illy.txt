a coloured-pencil and gouache drawing on deep indigo paper, photographed flat.
square, 1:1.

one figure alone, large in the frame: a person sitting curled up, knees drawn
to the chest, both arms wrapped around the shins, head tipped forward onto the
knees, long thin lines trailing down past the feet and off the bottom edge.

the figure is drawn as one continuous wobbling line in pale chalk, un-erased,
overlapping itself where the hand went round twice. over and around that line
the pencil has laid dry colour straight onto the dark paper: hot magenta
worked up one side of the body and under the arms, cold cyan down the other
side and through the hair, the two never blending, both left as visible pencil
strokes with the paper showing through. a little white gouache picks out the
hardest edges.

the indigo paper is the background and is left bare everywhere else — no scene,
no floor, no horizon. visible paper grain throughout.

no words anywhere in the picture.

NO photorealism, NO 3d rendering, NO chrome or metal, NO airbrush, NO smooth
gradients, NO glow, NO title lettering, NO artist name, NO logo, NO barcode,
NO signature, NO lettering of any kind.
