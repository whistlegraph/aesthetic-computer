a photograph of a colored-pencil and gouache drawing on deep indigo paper.
square, 1:1. seen from directly overhead, straight down, flat plan view.

the same dancefloor at the end of the night. thirty places on the grid, and
only five of them still lit.

each person is the same figure from the reference score, drawn as one
continuous white chalk line on the dark floor: sitting curled up, knees to the
chest, arms wrapped round the shins, head down on the knees, long thin lines
trailing past the feet. wobbling, un-erased, one unbroken stroke each, overlaps
visible. they are laid out on the score's own grid — six rows of five, evenly
spaced, none of them touching, none of them turned toward another.

light is diegetic and comes from the floor: a small coloured lamp set into the
boards beside each figure. five lamps are still burning — magenta, cyan,
chartreuse, amber, violet — each throwing a pool that reaches to the edge of
its own person and stops. the other twenty-five lamps are dark: small dull
glass discs in the boards, unlit. the figures beside them are still drawn, but
outside a pool the chalk reads only as faint cold grey, nearly lost in the
indigo. most of the picture is empty dark floor. the boards are visible in the
pools: plain worn wood, no pattern, no logo.

chalked on the floor beside four of the still-lit figures — one word each,
small, lowercase, in the same hand as the drawing:

  sitting · assise · сижу · बैठी

the room is emptying and nobody has moved. that is the club.
