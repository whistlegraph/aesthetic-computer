# Gunbunnies

Vertical felt-diorama shorts starring the recurring Aesthetic Computer bunny.

## The Dandelion Rifle

The bunny fires once. A target erupts into dandelions. The returning seeds
cover him while he remains professional.

Final: `out/dandelion-rifle-multipov.mp4`

| Beat | Camera | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aim | Range wide | `out/dandelion-rifle.mp4` |
| Fire | Bunny POV | `out/dandelion-rifle-pov.mp4` |
| Germinate | Target macro | `out/dandelion-rifle-impact.mp4` |
| Bloom | Bunny POV | `out/dandelion-rifle-pov.mp4` |
| Aftermath | Reaction close-up | `out/dandelion-rifle-reaction.mp4` |

All fal.ai shots use Seedance 2.0 Fast at 720p with native audio. Seeds and
motion prompts are recorded in `manifest.json`; `bin/cut-multipov.sh`
reproduces the final edit.

## Skate Cameras

The bunny rolls through a security corridor on a red felt skateboard and turns
three wall cameras into dandelion bursts with a compact felt gat.

Final: `out/skate-cameras-action.mp4`

The tracking, wheel, security-POV, and impact sources are recorded in
`skate-cameras-manifest.json`; `bin/cut-skate-cameras.sh` reproduces the edit.

## Physical Accuracy Proof

The push, planted recoil, and mounted-camera bloom remain ordered and readable.

Final: `out/physical-accuracy-proof.mp4`

The contracted Illys, contact maps, fal request IDs, seeds, and exact cuts are
recorded in `physical-accuracy-manifest.json`; `bin/cut-physical-accuracy.sh`
reproduces the edit.
