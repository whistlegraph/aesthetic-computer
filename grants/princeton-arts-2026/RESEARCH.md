## The offer

- Two consecutive 10-month academic years, 2027–28 and 2028–29 (Sept 1 – Jul 1).
- $93,000 salary/year, appointed as **Associate Professional Specialist**, with
  University benefits; mirrors mention ~$5k/yr research fund + ~$2k classroom fund.
- Teach one undergraduate course per semester, OR an artistic assignment
  (directing, ensemble, etc.) in lieu of a class at the program's discretion.
- Full-time residential commitment at Princeton; expected to be "full and
  active members of the community."

## THIS ROUND'S DISCIPLINE RESTRICTION (the eligibility crux)

The 2027–29 round is open to **dance, music, and theater artists only** — it is
explicitly *not* accepting creative writers or visual artists this cycle.
(Confirmed by two independent mirrors: ProFellow call-for-applications page and
contemporaryperformance.com, both dated Aug 2026.)

→ AC's viable lane is **music**: instrument-builder / composer / live-coding
performer. Not the visual-arts or writing framing.

## Eligibility rules (from mirrors — verify)

- "Early-career" artists of extraordinary promise; no fixed years-of-experience
  definition; practice should already be established with a clear voice.
- Open to all nationalities; U.S. citizenship not required.
- Lifetime cap: may apply **at most twice ever**.
- Ineligible: Princeton Ph.D. holders; past Hodder Fellows; anyone with a
  "sustained, continuous relationship with Princeton" (occasional connections
  OK); people pursuing a degree during the fellowship; people holding a
  concurrent teaching position elsewhere during the fellowship period.
