## The facts (confirmed live, 2026-09-23)

- **Program:** Eyebeam **Virtual Residency, Fall 2026**, under the 2026/2027
  season theme **AGENCY** ("What does it mean to have agency today?"). One of
  two cohorts this season; the other is the NYC Residency (Spring 2027,
  $5,000, NYC residents only, on site at Eyebeam weekly). "Applicants may
  only apply to one residency. The two tracks are defined by where you're
  based." Jeffrey is in Los Angeles, so **Virtual is the only track open to
  him.**
- **Deadline:** **Sunday, October 11, 2026, 11:59 PM Eastern** (8:59 PM
  Pacific). "no late applications will be accepted." Today is 2026-09-23, so
  **18 days out.**
- **Applications opened:** September 21, 2026.
- **Info session:** Zoom, **Monday, Sept 28, 2026, 1 PM Eastern** (10 AM
  Pacific). Register:
  https://us02web.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_cLRFdaeuQSqxFbNpjTT23Q
- **Interviews:** Oct 26-28, 2026 ("30-minute virtual interview with Eyebeam
  staff" for shortlisted applicants).
- **Notifications:** Oct 29, 2026, by email, to everyone.
- **Residency period:** **Nov 4, 2026 - Feb 12, 2027.** Described as a
  "12-week virtual residency"; the calendar span is ~14.5 weeks, so expect a
  holiday gap (unverified).
- **Cohort:** six US-based artists.
- **Fee:** none. "Applying to Eyebeam's open call is free." Via Submittable.
- **Format:** fully online. Minimum commitment **3-4 hours per week**,
  "including weekly cohort sessions plus their own independent time."

- "Process Presentation: Share research and process at a virtual event at

- "Publication Contribution: Develop text, imagery, or other material to
  contribute to an end-of-year publication"

FAQ: "Residents are not required to produce a finished project. Eyebeam's

The Virtual track "is well-suited to projects in the early stages of
development where the opportunity to take part in shared research, discussion
groups, and get feedback on nascent ideas would help advance concept
development and prototyping."

### The theme (verbatim, from /focus/agency and the open call)

"Who has agency and how is it enacted? How is our understanding of agency
being rewritten as 'AI agents' and 'agentic systems' increasingly act, make
decisions, and carry out tasks on our behalf?"

"We're looking for projects that explore:
- Who has agency? Who acts? And who is acted upon?
- What does agency become once it no longer belongs to a single actor (human
  or machine)?
- How to reimagine AI systems through the lens of collective governance,
  repair, rehearsal or mischief
- How to repurpose AI systems toward new tools, methods, and models for the
  public good"

"Throughout, the program positions artists as interlocutors of technology,
exploring its possibilities and pitfalls and proposing how these tools might
serve people over profit."

standardized scoring rubric. Our jury is made up of Eyebeam staff, industry
professionals, and Eyebeam alumni." No individual feedback.

## Fit and the spine

**Confidence: medium-high on eligibility, medium on selection.** Six seats,
national pool, no fee, so the pool will be large. The rubric is three lines:
timely take on AGENCY, execution ability, cohort contribution. The draft
has to win the first line; the CV and CultureHub recording carry the second;
NELA Computer Club and the UCLA residency carry the third.

**Chosen spine: the marked agent in a shared computer.** AC already has an
agent-presence system: an AI agent can be attached to a handle's room on the
session server, and every AC surface signed in as that handle lights a
"linked-agent mark" (`toolchain/mcp/acin-mcp.mjs`). That is a real, shipped,
unfinished thing. What does not exist yet: per-action attribution (which of
these keystrokes were the machine's), a room's ability to grant and revoke
what the agent may touch, and any public rehearsal of people and an agent
making pieces in the same space. Those three gaps are the residency. It hits
all four theme bullets literally (who acts; agency shared across a handle;
collective governance and mischief; repurposing an AI system toward a free
public tool), it is early-stage (the track's stated preference), and it is
prototypable at 3-4 hours a week from LA.

**Why not the CultureHub spine directly.** Fía is right that the shape is
similar (small stipend, cohort, public showing, tech support). But the
CultureHub piece is a *finished* laptop-ensemble performance, the AGENCY
call reads AI-first, and the virtual track wants nascent work. The
CultureHub recording becomes the **completed work sample** and the bio
carries over nearly verbatim. Backup spine if Jeffrey rejects the agent
angle: *Note(s)pat(ial) Native* reframed as "agency once it no longer belongs
to a single actor" (sound bodies migrating across nine machines, none of
which is in charge). Weaker on timeliness; stronger on portfolio.

**Facts used in the draft (canonical, from prior drafts):** AC five years
old, open source, 17,000+ user programs; KidLisp 118 built-ins; notepat, No
Paint, Whistlegraph; NELA Computer Club (Plot.Place, Chinatown LA);
collections of KADIST and SMK; UCLA Social Software with Casey Reas as
host; Yale MFA 2013, Ringling BFA 2011. **Do not** claim AC Native runs on
Apple hardware (see `culturehub-la-2026/acceptance/README.md`).

## Risks

1. **Residency-conflict clause vs. Matadero / Spain.** The biggest one. See
   above. Decide before Oct 11.
2. **"Years practicing" field.** No cap published this cycle, but the
   number (15+) will be visible to a jury that last year selected "first
   decade" artists. Lead with the project, not the CV.
3. **AI framing.** The FAQ says AI is not required, but three of four theme
   bullets name it. The spine uses a language-model agent honestly; do not
   name a vendor in the proposal, say "a language-model agent."
4. **$2,000 for 12 weeks at 3-4 hrs/week** is roughly $40-55/hour before any
   withholding. Fine as a network play (Kaganskiy, Zicarelli, Nasser, the
   alumni mentor pool, the publication), not as income.
5. **Video introduction** is a required upload. Needs a quiet room and one
   take; do it the week of Sept 28, not Oct 10.
6. **Work-sample links must stay live ~90 days past Oct 11.** Use
   YouTube/Vimeo unlisted, not a self-hosted URL that may move.
