## The facts (confirmed live, 2026-09-24)

- **What:** STAY Arts (Southeast LA arts nonprofit) is curating a
  **permanent art collection for the Kaiser Permanente Cudahy Medical
  Offices**. Selected works are "professionally reproduced and installed
  throughout the clinic" as canvas, framed, or acrylic prints.
- **Deadline:** **Sunday, September 27, 2026 at 11:59 PM** (Pacific
  implied; STAY Arts is in LA). Today is 2026-09-24, so **3 days out.**
- **Money:** **$500 licensing honorarium per artwork selected**. Up to
  **5 artworks** per artist, so the ceiling is $2,500. Artist keeps
  ownership and copyright; signs a licensing agreement for reproduction
  and display. No original is acquired.
- **Eligibility:** "local artists" in visual art: painting, photography,
  illustration, drawing, printmaking, **digital art**, mixed media, "other
  two-dimensional artwork suitable for professional reproduction."
  "Artists with connections to Cudahy, Southeast Los Angeles, and
  surrounding communities are especially encouraged." No hard residency
  gate stated.
- **Existing work is fine:** "New work does not need to be created for this
  opportunity."
- **Context constraint:** must be "suitable for ... permanent display in a
  healthcare environment." Read: calm, legible, no text-heavy or
  distressing imagery.
- **Timeline:** curatorial review late Sept through early Oct; artist
  notifications "to be announced."
- **If selected, digital work:** supply a "full-resolution production file."
  Physical work: bring the original to STAY Arts for photography.
