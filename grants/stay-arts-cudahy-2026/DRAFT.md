## Page 2 — Artist Background
**Primary Artistic Medium(s)** (multi-select): Digital Art · Painting · Drawing

**Artist Bio**
Jeffrey Alan Scudder is a Los Angeles artist and programmer. He builds
Aesthetic Computer, a small free creative operating system for drawing,
sound, and play, and makes his pictures on phones, tablets, and software he writes himself.
*Radical Digital Painting*, 239 pictures painted 2016–2021, was exhibited
at Johannes Vogt Gallery in New York and toured as a lecture-performance; he has performed at CultureHub LA and shown with the Internet
Archive, Rhizome, and the New Museum. He has taught at CalArts and UCLA.

**Connection to Cudahy / SELA** (long answer)
I live and work in Los Angeles. I don't have a Cudahy address, so I'll be
honest about the connection: it's the audience. My pictures are made to be
looked at slowly by people who did not come to see art, and a clinic waiting
room is exactly that. The work is bright, soft-edged, and abstract; children
find animals in it and adults find weather. I'd be glad to license any of
these five to hang where people are nervous and waiting, and I'm happy to
supply print-resolution files for whatever sizes the walls want.
