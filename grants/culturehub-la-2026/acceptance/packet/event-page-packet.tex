\pageclaim{Six salvaged laptops become one spatial instrument, played from inside the score.}

Jeffrey Alan Scudder expands \emph{Special Sign}, his 1:41 composition for twelve
moving sound bodies, into a live performance on six aging laptops revived with
AC Native. The computers form a distributed instrument across CultureHub LA’s
surround system. Scudder rotates the entire system, changes its speed and
trajectory, and moves individual voices among the machines while the audience
sits inside the score. The performance culminates a ten-day residency devoted
to rebuilding the fixed recording as a longer form that can change each time it
is played. A conversation follows the approximately 30-minute performance.
