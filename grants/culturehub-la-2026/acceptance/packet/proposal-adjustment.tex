\quoteblock{At CultureHub I'm presenting two new pieces, one for AC OS and one
for macOS. \emph{Note(s)pat(ial)} spreads notepat across six surplus grade-school
laptops, each booted into AC OS, the operating system I make; from a live control
surface I turn the whole sound field through the room. \emph{The MacNeoPolitan
Trio} is newer and still taking shape — three MacBook Neos, each played through
Menu Band, my menu-bar piano. One runs on machines pulled back from e-waste, the
other on machines bought new.}%
{82 words. The first title still contains the one they accepted, so the programme
reads as an expansion of \emph{Notepat Jam} rather than a replacement for it.
``Still taking shape'' keeps the second half free to change.}

\vspace{0.9em}
{\normalfont\bfseries\fontsize{12.5pt}{15pt}\selectfont\color{acdark}B — fallback\par}
\vspace{0.35em}
\quoteblock{At CultureHub I'm turning six salvaged laptops into one instrument.
Each boots into AC Native, the operating system I make, and takes a single
channel of the studio's surround system, so the audience sits inside the sound
rather than facing it. From a live control surface I rotate the whole field
through the room, change its speed, and move voices between machines. The
instruments are rescued, not bought — I want a computer to hold an instrument
the way it holds a clock.}%
{83 words. Keeps the ecological answer intact and names no work title, so the
second half can arrive later. A retreat: it hides a decision already made, and
the set list would then contradict the artist page.}
