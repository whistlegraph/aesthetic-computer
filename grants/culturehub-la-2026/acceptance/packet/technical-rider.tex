\section{System}
\begin{itemize}
\item Six artist-supplied laptops booted from six AC Native USB drives.
\item Independent sound bodies distributed among the six laptops.
\item Live control of system rotation, trajectory, speed, and voice routing.
\item Local wired or isolated Wi-Fi network for clock, transport, and control.
\item Six discrete audio feeds to the venue’s spatial system, subject to the verified 5.1 topology.
\item House projection showing the live spatial score.
\end{itemize}

\clearpage
\packettitle{House request}{Audio · network · projection · room}

\begin{itemize}
\item Kalio 5.1 system with independently addressable inputs; confirm whether the sixth path is full-range or LFE-only.
\item Audio interface or interfaces providing six line-level outputs, with suitable DI boxes, cabling, and adapters.
\item Projector, screen or projection wall, and HDMI input near the performance position.
\item Dedicated router or switch isolated from public traffic.
\item Six laptop power positions and conditioned power for audio/network gear.
\item Six low tables or plinths around the audience field.
\item Central seating for approximately 20–40 people, subject to room capacity and accessible routes.
\item Separate stereo livestream/documentation feed derived from the spatial mix.
\item One technical rehearsal with CultureHub’s audio and streaming staff.
\end{itemize}
