\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}
The swap is hardware, not a patch, and it happens in front of the audience: it
needs budgeted minutes and a decision about what the room does meanwhile —
lights and talking, a held drone, or the score on the projector. Six line-level
outs cover both pieces if the patch can change during it; nine if not. The
instruments stay on afterward, so nothing is struck until the room clears.
\end{minipage}\hfill
