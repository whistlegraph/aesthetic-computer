\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}
I take six machines off and put three on, in front of everyone. The guest set
used to hide that, and I cut the guest. Give it five to ten minutes and tell me
what the room should be doing while it happens — house lights and talking is
fine, or I leave a drone running, or the score goes up on the projector. Six
line outs are enough if we can repatch mid-show. Nine if we can't. Afterward
people play the instruments themselves, so don't strike anything until the room
empties.

\vspace{0.20em}
\textbf{What I'm worried about.} Citrus is my development machine, 8 GB, and it
will hang if I let it, so it performs from a clean boot or I bring a fourth Neo.
There is no third Neo yet. And drift doesn't announce itself — a smeared
downbeat still plays, it just sounds wrong, so we check it at the rehearsal on
your real network.
