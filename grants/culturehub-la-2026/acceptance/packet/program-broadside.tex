\setitem{1}{Note(s)pat(ial) Native}{Six surplus grade-school laptops · AC OS · notepat}{${\sim}$15 min}
{A spatial composition for twelve moving sound bodies, grown live from a fixed
1:41 recording. Each laptop is a surplus grade-school machine given a second life,
and each takes one channel of the room. From a live control surface the whole field turns,
changes speed, and hands its voices between machines. The audience sits inside
it.}

\setitem{2}{Guest set}{To be confirmed}{${\sim}$15 min}
{One invited performer, chosen for the room rather than the roster. Held open
until the invitation is made and accepted — nothing here is promised yet.}

\setitem{3}{The MacNeoPolitan Trio}{Three MacBook Neos · macOS · Menu Band}{${\sim}$15 min}
{Indigo, citrus, blush — Neapolitan, in a block. Each machine is played through
Menu Band, where the keyboard sits in the menu bar beside the clock, the network,
and the battery. A newer work, still taking shape.}

\setitem{4}{Conversation / free play}{Everyone}{30--45 min}
{The argument the evening is making: an instrument belongs inside a computer the
way a clock does — not an application you go out and get, but a built-in facility.
The instruments stay on, and the room is invited to play them.}

\textbf{Notes for the run.} \emph{Note(s)pat(ial) Native} boots AC OS from USB and
changes nothing on any disk; \emph{The MacNeoPolitan Trio} runs Menu Band, a
shipping universal macOS app — AC Native is x86\_64 UEFI only and cannot boot on
Apple hardware. The changeover between sets 1 and 3 is a hardware swap rather
than a patch change, and the guest set covers it.\par}
