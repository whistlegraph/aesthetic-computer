\packettitle{What was submitted}{\emph{Notepat Jam}\dsep 21 May 2026\dsep the version they accepted}

\pageclaim{One work on six rescued laptops\ddash one laptop per surround
channel\ddash and an ecological claim made structurally rather than
rhetorically}

\section{The work and the two public events}
A six-channel piece performed solo on six aging laptops — donated, thrifted,
e-waste-bound — each revived into AC Native, each running notepat and voicing one
discrete channel of the 5:1 Kalio surround. Roughly thirty minutes, the audience
seated inside the field. Before it, open programming: anyone brings a laptop and
boots it into AC Native, and a dead machine becomes an instrument in an hour.
Then the concert, livestreamed.

\section{How it answered the call}
{\tighttable
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{L{1.55in} Y}
\toprule

\textbf{Ecological responsibility} & ``The six instruments are revived e-waste. \textbf{No new equipment is fabricated for the show} — the sustainability is structural, not a statement.'' \\
\textbf{Localized networks} & Six laptops synced over the studio's own local network — ``no internet, no cloud.'' \\
\textbf{Human connection} & Audience inside the field; the open session hands the method back to the room. \\

\section{What was also promised}
The multitrack and the AC Native image released open-source, so the method

% ─────────────────────────────────────────── now (same sheet as submitted)
\midtitle{What is prepared now}{AC PRESENTS 2 NEW PIECES\dsep titles set 11 August 2026}

\pageclaim{Two works and nine laptops\ddash half the program is hardware
bought new and a second instrument enters that the application never named}

\section{The two works}
\textbf{AC OS Special Sign.} The six salvaged laptops remain on AC Native, but
the piece is now the expanded live version of \emph{Special Sign} — a spatial
composition for twelve moving sound bodies, grown from a fixed 1:41 recording.
The field rotates, changes speed, and hands voices between machines.

\textbf{The MacNeoPolitan Trio.} New. Three MacBook Neos — indigo, citrus,
blush — each played through Menu Band, the menu-bar piano. It runs Menu Band, a
shipping universal macOS app: AC Native is x86\_64 UEFI only and cannot boot on
Apple Silicon, and no copy should ever claim otherwise.

\section{The offering and the billing}
Roughly forty minutes across the two works with a guest slot between them, then a
conversation. The participation session is now the \textbf{Menu Band Jam} — bring
a Mac, install Menu Band — with the AC Native USB boot offered as an alternative.
Billed \textbf{AC PRESENTS 2 NEW PIECES}, replacing the
\emph{Whistlegraph presents} line of 9 August.

\section{The ecological answer needs a decision rather than a repair}
The submitted text made ecology structural: nothing new is built for this show.
Half the new program is bought-new hardware. \texttt{ARTIST-PAGE.md} already
answers this deliberately — the two works are ``the same instrument on opposite
ends of a computer's life'' — which is a strong reading. It is still a revision
of an answer that was graded, and it is better said than discovered.

\section{The public page still shows the old program}
\texttt{system/public/culture-hub-2026/} reads \emph{Notepat Jam · submitted} and
now contradicts the program. The embargo forbids publishing the new one before
CultureHub's late-August announcement, so this needs a plan, not a push.

\section{A\ddash recommended\dsep both works with soft edges}
\quoteblock{At CultureHub I'm presenting two works for laptop ensemble.
\emph{AC OS Special Sign} spreads a spatial composition across six salvaged
laptops, each booted into AC Native, the operating system I make; from a live
control surface I turn the whole sound field through the room. \emph{The
MacNeoPolitan Trio} is newer and still taking shape — three MacBook Neos, each
played through Menu Band, my menu-bar piano. One work runs on machines pulled
back from e-waste, the other on machines bought new.}%
{81 words. Names both works under the new titles. ``Still taking shape'' does real work:
it earns the right to change the second half without contradicting a published
quotation, which matters because the blush Neo is not bought yet. Sets up the
salvaged-versus-new reading rather than leaving it to be noticed.}

\section{B\ddash fallback\dsep the accepted spine only}
\quoteblock{At CultureHub I'm turning six salvaged laptops into one instrument.
Each boots into AC Native, the operating system I make, and takes a single
channel of the studio's surround system, so the audience sits inside the sound
rather than facing it. From a live control surface I rotate the whole field
through the room, change its speed, and move voices between machines. The
instruments are rescued, not bought — I want a computer to hold an instrument
the way it holds a clock.}%
{83 words. Holds the spine they accepted and keeps the ecological answer
intact. Names no work title at all, so the second half can arrive later. Kept
here only as a retreat: it hides a decision already made, and the set list would
then contradict the artist page.}

At this length neither statement can explain the change. That belongs in a short
separate note to Mattie, or nowhere.
