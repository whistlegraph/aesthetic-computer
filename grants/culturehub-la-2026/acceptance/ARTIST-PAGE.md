# Jeffrey Alan Scudder

## Bio

Jeffrey Alan Scudder is a Los Angeles–based artist, educator, and technologist
who builds musical instruments and creative tools. He created Aesthetic
Computer, Whistlegraph, and AC Native, a bootable operating system that turns
aging laptops into instruments. Scudder holds an MFA from Yale and is an Author
in Residence at UCLA Social Software. He hosts NELA Computer Club in Chinatown,
Los Angeles. His work is held in the collections of KADIST and SMK, the National
Gallery of Denmark.

## Project statement

During the CultureHub residency, I’m developing an expanded live version of
*Special Sign*, my spatial composition for twelve moving sound bodies. Six
salvaged laptops booted into AC Native become a distributed instrument inside
CultureHub LA’s surround system. From a live control surface, I can rotate the
entire system, change its speed and trajectory, and redistribute its voices
among the computers. The residency lets the 1:41 recorded work unfold as a
longer, variable performance whose spatial form is played in real time.
