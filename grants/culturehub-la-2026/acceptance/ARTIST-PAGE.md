# Jeffrey Alan Scudder

## Bio

Jeffrey Alan Scudder is a Los Angeles–based artist, educator, and technologist
who builds musical instruments and creative tools. He created Aesthetic
Computer, Whistlegraph, AC Native — a bootable operating system that turns aging
laptops into instruments — and Menu Band, a piano that lives in the macOS menu
bar. Scudder holds an MFA from Yale and is an Author in Residence at UCLA Social
Software. He hosts NELA Computer Club in Chinatown, Los Angeles. His work is
held in the collections of KADIST and SMK, the National Gallery of Denmark.

## Project statement

During the CultureHub residency I'm building a two-work program for laptop
ensemble.

*Special Sign* expands my spatial composition for twelve moving sound bodies.
Six salvaged laptops, booted into AC Native, become one distributed instrument
inside CultureHub LA's surround system. From a live control surface I rotate the
whole field, change its speed and trajectory, and redistribute its voices among
the computers.

*MacNeoPolitan* is a spatial audio play for three MacBook Neos — indigo,
citrus, and blush — each one played through Menu Band, my menu-bar piano.

One work runs on machines pulled back from e-waste; the other on machines bought
new. Both insist that an instrument belongs inside a computer the way a clock
does — not as an application you go out and get, but as a built-in facility,
sitting in the menu bar beside the time, the network, and the battery.
