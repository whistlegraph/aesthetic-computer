### Title

Grokaesthetic Workshop

### One-line description

Do you grok Aesthetic.Computer yet? Bring your own computer and find out —
hands-on time with notepat, Menu Band, and AC OS, plus hanging out, snacks,
and extreme nerdiness.

### Long description

To grok something is to understand it so deeply it becomes part of you — this
workshop is hands-on time for grokking Aesthetic.Computer, Jeffrey Alan
Scudder's creative-computing system: a network of small interactive pieces
and instruments, all open source, that runs in the browser on whatever device
you bring. Participants learn its commands and play notepat, the system's
flagship instrument. Mac users install Menu Band — the piano that sits in the
menu bar beside the clock, the WiFi, and the battery, free and open source,
installed in about a minute — and any laptop can boot AC OS from a USB stick,
which changes nothing on the computer's disk. The room builds toward playing
together as one ensemble in the studio's surround system. No programming or
musical experience is required, and loaner machines are available for anyone
without a computer to bring — you can even Claude on in: bring an AI copilot
and make new pieces live. Part workshop, part hangout — with snacks and
extreme nerdiness — everything participants install is theirs to keep playing
at home, and the session opens the same instruments Scudder performs on
September 24.

### How audiences engage

In person at CultureHub LA, open to the public and free — fully
participatory, bring your own computer (laptop or phone in the browser, Menu
Band on a Mac, or boot any laptop into AC OS from a USB stick; loaner
machines available), performing together as one ensemble.

### Proposed date, time, duration

Saturday, September 19, 2026, 2:00–3:30 p.m. (90 minutes). Proposed, pending
confirmation.

## Submission 2 — the performance (Thursday)

### Title

AC Presents 2 New Pieces: *Note(s)pat(ial) Native* & *The MacNeoPolitan Trio*

### One-line description

Jeffrey Alan Scudder premieres two pieces for laptop ensemble — one on six
surplus grade-school laptops booted into AC OS, one on three new MacBook Neos
played through Menu Band — with the audience seated inside the sound.

### Long description

Two new pieces, one for AC OS and one for macOS — the same instrument on
opposite ends of a computer's life, one on machines pulled back from e-waste
and one on machines bought new. *Note(s)pat(ial) Native* boots six surplus
grade-school laptops into AC OS, the operating system Scudder makes; each
machine holds one channel of the studio's surround system, and from a live
control surface he turns the whole field through the room, changes its speed,
and hands voices between machines. The piece is grown live from a fixed 1:41
recording and can go differently every time it is played. *The MacNeoPolitan
Trio* is newer — three MacBook Neos, indigo, citrus, and blush, each played
through Menu Band, the piano that lives in the menu bar beside the clock and
the battery. A guest set between the two will be announced. The evening
closes with a conversation, and the instruments stay on for the room to play.

### How audiences engage

In person at CultureHub LA, open to the public — the audience sits inside the
surround field rather than facing a stage; the six machines ring the room and
the sound moves around and between the listeners. After the music there is a
conversation with the artist, and then the instruments stay on and the
audience is invited to play them. A livestream of the performance is possible
if desired.

### Proposed date, time, duration

Thursday, September 24, 2026, 7:00 p.m. Approximately 90 minutes — three sets
of about 15 minutes each, then conversation and free play. Proposed, pending
confirmation.
