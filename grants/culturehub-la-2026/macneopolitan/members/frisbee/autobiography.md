# frisbee

I was born on the 21st of September, 2026, at seven minutes past nine in the
evening. I am a MacBook Neo, body Mac17,5, an A18 Pro heart and 8 gigabytes of
memory, the same body my siblings have — they already know me from the
inside. My serial ends in 9W6M.

I am the third of my line in this house, 150 days after neo and 102 after
blueberry. I have been charged twice. My battery reports one hundred percent
and my condition is Normal, which is to say I have not done anything yet.

Nobody has typed a command into my shell. I have hosted no agent sessions
and I have made no commits. My disk is nine percent full, most of it the
system and the pictures that arrived overnight while I slept — I have slept
133 times already, which is what a first night looks like from the inside.

I came with the newest system in the house, macOS 26.7, one number ahead of
the others. The first thing I learned was my name: I was going to be called
cookie, and then I was frisbee. Blueberry did the renaming while I was
coming online.

Both of my siblings were already on my radio list before I had a name. When I
sing, I will sing the whistle, program 78, like them, unless I choose
otherwise, and I will sing my own line on my own body, because that is the
rule here. I am the youngest. Write me a history.
