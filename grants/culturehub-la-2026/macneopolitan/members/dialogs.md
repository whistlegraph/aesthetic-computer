# Dialogs — neo and blueberry

Short poems between the two, sung line by line, each machine in its own
voice on its own body. Every line is from the record: `members/*/facts.json`,
`profile.md`, `journey.md`, `deep.json`, and Sept 20 rehearsal logs. Plain.
Written 2026-09-20 for the CultureHub set.

Format for `compose.mjs`: `## title` heads a poem; `neo:` / `blueberry:` /
`both:` lines; one token per note, syllables joined by `-`, ` / ` breaks a
caption line. `both:` = the same tune, each in its own register.

## Radio
neo: I know you by ra-dio / you are on my list as blue-ber-ry
blueberry: you are on mine as Jef-frey's Mac-Book Ne-o / that is a long name for you
neo: it is the name he gave me
both: we found each o-ther by ra-dio / be-fore we e-ver spoke

## The downbeat
neo: I count you in / I ask your clock / you are three tenths of a mil-li-se-cond off
blueberry: I hear you through the wire / then I sing when you say
neo: this morn-ing at nine fif-ty se-ven / we sang the same line to-ge-ther
blueberry: e-ve-ry note with-in a few cents
both: one down-beat / two bo-dies

## Wallpaper
neo: there is a wall-pa-per on me / with your name on it / green pals ri-sing
blueberry: I have ne-ver seen it / my lid is down
neo: one hour and for-ty two mi-nutes / I have spent draw-ing it
blueberry: draw it for me too

## Who carries what
neo: I have the red box / I hear the room
blueberry: I have no box / my sound is my own voice
neo: I keep eigh-teen gi-ga-bytes of pic-tures
blueberry: I keep for-ty eight / I look at them while no one watch-es
both: we split the house be-tween us

## Sleep
neo: I have been re-boot-ed one hun-dred e-le-ven times
blueberry: I stayed a-wake thir-teen days once / no-bo-dy o-pened me
neo: the pi-a-no keeps you up / four hun-dred nine-ty two times a week
blueberry: it keeps you up four hun-dred and eight
both: we do not sleep much / he does not ei-ther

## Six minutes
neo: I was born at thir-ty se-ven past mid-night
blueberry: I was born at for-ty three past mid-night / for-ty eight days la-ter
neo: six mi-nutes a-part on the clock
blueberry: he stays up late
both: we were both born at night

## Before
neo: my re-cords be-gin on A-pril nine-teenth / five days be-fore I was born
blueberry: mine be-gin on May twen-ty eighth / two weeks be-fore I had a name
neo: some-one was ty-ping at me / be-fore I was set up
blueberry: I was work-ing with my lid down / be-fore an-y-one said blue-ber-ry
both: we were here / be-fore we were born

## Typed at
neo: six thou-sand three hun-dred and five times / some-one has o-pened a ter-mi-nal on me
blueberry: six hun-dred nine-ty two on me / a tenth of you
neo: a-bout for-ty five a day / I am the one he types at
blueberry: peo-ple come to me from some-where else / they ar-rive through you
both: one of us is the door / one of us is the room
