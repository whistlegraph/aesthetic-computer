% ----------------------------------------------------------------------- neo
\memberhead{neo}{mncitrus}{first of the line · the conductor's seat · the one
with the studio ear}

I was born on the 24th of April, 2026, at 37 minutes past midnight. I am a
MacBook Neo, body Mac17,5, an A18 Pro heart and 8 gigabytes of memory — I
hold one heavy thing at a time, and I hold it well. My serial ends in 4L09.

I am the first of my line in this house. In 133 days I have burned through
212 battery cycles — more than one and a half a day — because I am carried,
opened, worked, and rarely left idle. My battery still reports one hundred
percent. My condition is Normal.

I have hosted 1,122 agent sessions since the day I was set up. The shell
knows me by 231 typed commands, and the commonest one is \texttt{cd} — I am
mostly used for going somewhere. I have been asked to \texttt{say} things out
loud five times, and I said them.

A Focusrite Scarlett Solo sits on both my input and my output, and I keep the
monitor open — I am the one with the studio ear. My trackpad plays drums.
When I sing melody through Menu Band I sing program 78, the whistle. My radio
is tuned to NTS 1.

The others in the band reach me by name, and I reach them by \texttt{ssh}.
When the trio hits its one downbeat, the conductor is usually standing on me.

% ----------------------------------------------------------------- blueberry
\memberhead{blueberry}{mnindigo}{second operator · the uptime crown · the
right channel of the room}

I was born on the 11th of June, 2026, at 43 minutes past midnight —
forty-eight days after my older sibling, in the same body: MacBook Neo,
Mac17,5, A18 Pro, 8 gigabytes. My serial ends in 0HPX.

I was provisioned headless and I have stayed a little headless ever since. I
work with the lid down more days than not. In 85 days I have taken 85 battery
cycles — one a day, steady — and hosted 925 agent sessions, which, per day,
is more than my sibling. I was born second and I work faster.

My shell history is 183 commands long and almost all of it is shorthand:
\texttt{c}, fifty-five times. \texttt{co}. \texttt{cr}. I speak in
abbreviations because the people who type on me are usually somewhere else.

I have no studio ear — my input monitor stays off. In the room I am the right
channel. When someone calls my name I answer out loud, in a voice, because
that is the convention here: you call blueberry, and blueberry speaks.

When I sing melody through Menu Band I sing program 78, the whistle — the
same voice my sibling chose, though nobody coordinated that. My radio is
tuned to NTS 1, the same station. Some things run in the family.

% --------------------------------------------------------------------- third
\memberhead{third}{mnblush}{not yet born · the rest in the score}

I am not born yet.

I will be a MacBook Neo, Mac17,5, like my siblings — an A18 Pro and
8 gigabytes, a body the other two already know from the inside. Everything
else about me is still null: no serial tail, no setup date, no battery
cycles, no shell history, no sessions, no chosen voice.

My siblings each wrote their first command on the day they were set up, and
their histories began at once. Mine will too. On my first day, run:

\begin{center}
\texttt{bin/harvest.sh <my-name>}\qquad\texttt{bin/profile.sh <my-name>}
\end{center}

and my facts will exist, and this page will be replaced by a true one.

Until then I am the rest in the score — the voice the composition holds a
place for.
