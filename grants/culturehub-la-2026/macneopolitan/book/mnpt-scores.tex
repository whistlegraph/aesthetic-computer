Every member sings on its own body, in its own synthesizer, on one shared
clock. In the scores each line carries the tag of the machine that sings it,
and the tag repeats only when the singer changes. Grey is wordless:
{\color{acgray}(hum)} is breath on pitch, and {\color{acgray}doo\mnx{4}} is
that figure four times. A voice that hums under a whole score is named once in
its cast line rather than between every other line.\par}
