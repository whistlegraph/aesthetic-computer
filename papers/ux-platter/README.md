# UX — platter

A reading platter for **interface persuasion**: how color, position, labels and
defaults steer a choice among priced options, and how strong the evidence for
each is. A sub-platter within the [papers platter](../SCORE.md), opened
2026-11-18 for a study of pricing-tier color.

## Posture

**Index and citation only.** No source text or page image is reproduced in this
repo. Quote briefly and name the source. Bibliography with URLs and
verification status: [`sources.json`](sources.json). Readings:
[`digest.md`](digest.md). Observations of public pricing pages:
[`pricing-pages.csv`](pricing-pages.csv).

Client work that draws on this platter stays in the vault. Nothing here names a
client's numbers or proposals.

## Shelves

| Shelf | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **attention** | Where the eye goes while choosing: salience, size, position, order. |
| **color** | What hue, saturation and lightness do to price perception and choice, with the replications and reversals. |
| **choice** | Labels, defaults, decoys and dark patterns, measured on real purchases where possible. |
| **practice** | Practitioner tests and counts of pricing pages. Lower-grade evidence, labelled as such. |
| **regulation** | Where visual steering becomes a deceptive practice. |
| **access** | Contrast and color-vision limits any palette must meet. |

## Grades

Each digest entry carries one grade.

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **strong** | Meta-analysis, or replicated across teams. |
| **moderate** | Several studies, or one randomized field experiment on real purchases. |
| **weak** | One study, one team, or a vendor test. |
| **folklore** | Asserted without data. |

## Capture

Pricing pages are observed logged out, from a rendered page, with the capture
date in the CSV. A count is only as good as its render: a page read from markup
alone is marked `markup` and wants a rendered pass.
