# 02 · Spatial antiphony: placement rules for a five-plus-one ring

Shelf: spatial · Sources: `sources-02.json`

Companion to `grants/culturehub-la-2026/chamber-studies/02-spatial-antiphony.md` (hexagon). Layout here: five laptops in a pentagon (seat 1 front, clockwise) plus a sixth at the center on a small speaker, held by the performer.

## Unit

At 112 BPM a beat is 0.536 s, a bar 2.143 s. In a pentagon 5 to 7 m across, any two speakers are under 12 ms apart, so simultaneous onsets fuse and onsets over 40 ms apart read as two events. One hop per beat is an orbit of 2.68 s; per eighth 1.34 s; per bar 10.7 s. A step of two visits all five seats (1, 3, 5, 2, 4). No seat is opposite another; the rear pair 3 and 4 sit 144 degrees from seat 1.

## Facts

Polychoral psalms alternate verses between choirs, elide verse ends into the next entry, and unite at climaxes (`willaert-1550-salmi`). Zarlino wants each choir harmonically complete alone; St Mark's choirs often stood close, so phrasing carried the effect (`bryant-1981-cori-spezzati`). A short tutti refrain returns between solo sections (`gabrieli-1615-in-ecclesiis`). Brant separates only contrasting lines (`brant-1979-progress-report`).

Gruppen passes one process among three orchestras (`stockhausen-1957-gruppen`); its brass leaps right, center, left; it ends on tremolo strings, a cowbell, one wind note (`chang-2014-gruppen`). Kontakte's spun speaker (`stockhausen-1960-kontakte`) blurs to wah-wah and Doppler at speed; pauses hold the form (`stockhausen-essays-electronic`).

Terretektorh opens with three minutes of one note passed among 88 players seated with the audience (`xenakis-1966-terretektorh`). Persephassa accelerates six surrounding percussionists to one rotation per second, cut by silences (`xenakis-1969-persephassa`). Répons: ensemble center, six soloists around, entering later (`boulez-1984-repons`). Forty Part Motet: one voice per speaker, 3 of 14 loop minutes silent (`cardiff-2001-forty-part-motet`). PLOrk ties each sound to a seated person (`trueman-2006-plork-icmc`). Circular motion is followable to about 2.5 to 2.8 rot/s for noise, less for tones, least at high pitch (`feron-2010-rotation-limits`).

## Rules

R1 hop, do not glide, until Climb. A hop is a hard pin, crossfade under 30 ms; longer makes a phantom between seats. Glide only for orbits of 2.7 s or more.

R2 orbit period 2.7 to 10.7 s reads as motion; 0.7 to 1.4 s as spin, fine for noise, marginal for sines; under 0.5 s as modulation. Orbit low sines and triangles; noise spins fastest (`feron-2010-rotation-limits`).

R3 Appear: center only for 32 bars. Bar 33: seats 3 and 4 answer with one 4 bar phrase, then 1 bar silent (`boulez-1984-repons`, `xenakis-1966-terretektorh`).

R4 Ring: one 4 bar phrase per seat clockwise 1 to 5, 20 bars per circuit; center holds a low sine drone (`trueman-2006-plork-icmc`).

R5 Echoes: ring calls 2 bars, center answers 2 bars varied (triangle for sine, a third lower, 6 dB softer), each side harmonically complete; second half, the answer starts on the call's last beat (`willaert-1550-salmi`, `bryant-1981-cori-spezzati`). Every 16 bars all six sound one chord 2 bars, then 1 bar silent (`gabrieli-1615-in-ecclesiis`). Once per 8 bars a center note echoes at seats 1, 3, 5, a beat later and 6 dB quieter each.

R6 Climb: one voice orbits clockwise, hop rate stepping every 8 bars: bar, half note, beat, eighth; harmony pinned, register rising with rate (`xenakis-1969-persephassa`).

R7 Converge: last 2 beats of Climb spin under 0.5 s per orbit, 1 beat silent, then the D chord from all six at once. Blur once, as cadence (`stockhausen-essays-electronic`).

R8 Lift: one voice pinned per seat (`cardiff-2001-forty-part-motet`); two voices counter-rotate, one orbit per 16 bars. Climax: a chord in pentagram order 1, 3, 5, 2, 4 on downbeats, then 2 bars tutti (`chang-2014-gruppen`).

R9 Return: R4 reversed 5 to 1, dropping a voice per hop; rests grow 1 beat, 2 beats, 1 bar, 2 bars, longest after seat 3.

R10 Vanish: penultimate phrase from seats 3 and 4, final tone from the center alone, 8 bar fade, 2 bars silent (`chang-2014-gruppen`).

R11 one moving voice at a time except in Lift; every section keeps a 2 bar silence and a solo seat.
