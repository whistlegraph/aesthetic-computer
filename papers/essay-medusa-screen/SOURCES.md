# Sources — The Screen Looks Back

Checked August 5, 2026. The essay paraphrases its sources and does not reproduce
the Artforum article.

- Jan Tumlir and Jeffrey Stuker, “From Medusa to Nvidia: On the Algorithm’s
  Primal Scene,” *Artforum*, August 5, 2026:
  <https://www.artforum.com/features/jan-tumlir-jeffrey-stuker-algorithm-1234756309/>.
  Canonical source for the Medusa, mirror-shield, technical-gaze, algorithmic
  image, and Nvidia argument. Artforum’s public WordPress record supplied the
  publication and modification timestamps and identifies this as the first of
  four announced “Algorithm” entries.

  `system/public/papers.aesthetic.computer/platter/readings/tumlir-stuker-medusa-nvidia-2026.txt`.

  `papers/arxiv-ac/ac.tex`. Used for the immediate-mode runtime, prompt, handles,

- *The URL Tradition*:
  `papers/arxiv-url-tradition/url-tradition.tex`. Used for direct address, the
  prompt-as-address-bar model, and the absence of a recommender at the front
  door.
- *Sucking on the Complex*:
  `papers/arxiv-complex/complex.tex`. Used for instrument versus feed,
  anti-environments, platform attention, and the engine/userdom distinction.
- *Pieces Not Programs*:
  `papers/arxiv-pieces/pieces.tex`. Used for the piece as authored entry, named
  address, performance instance, and durable record.
- Runtime evidence:
  `system/public/aesthetic.computer/lib/disk.mjs` and

The essay makes a normative argument from these sources. Its proposed boundaries
for direct address, finite use, telemetry, retention, and generative-image
provenance are recommendations, not descriptions of every current AC surface.
