choir is an invite-only chat for artists, started by Fía Benitez for women artists in Los Angeles.

One main room. Post a thought, a photo, a picture from the camera, a PDF, or a voice note; voice notes come with a written transcript. Any message can grow its own thread. Tag a message to find it later.

Discover collects art news, open calls and openings, chosen daily. Each item has its own thread for talking it over.

Notifications for the chat and for Discover switch on and off separately.

Sign in with your email and a six-digit code. No password.

Members each get five invites. The room has rules (choir.ac/terms): long-press any message to report it or to hide its author, and every report is reviewed within 24 hours.
