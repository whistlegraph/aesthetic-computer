oskiewar is a stick-figure fight in a park, and you can bring people into it.

You walk in through the front doors. Inside there's a pool, a gym, ramps, and a long go-kart circuit, plus whatever is lying around: skateboards, a onewheel, karts, chalk, axes, grenades, guns. Pick something up and use it. Punch, kick, throw up a bubble to shield yourself, grab somebody and throw them. Limbs come off.

PLAYING WITH PEOPLE
Every room has an address. Send it to someone and they open it in a web browser — no install, no account — and walk into the same park, or take the second chair in a fight. Once a fight has two players, anyone else who arrives watches. Both machines run the whole fight and keep agreeing with each other frame by frame, so it stays responsive even when the connection isn't perfect.

CONTROLS
Keyboard: WASD or the arrow keys move. Q and E punch — hold one to grab. Shift kicks, Space jumps, Shift+Q raises the bubble, R drops what you're carrying.
Controller: the stick moves, X and Y punch, A kicks, B jumps, A and X together for the bubble.
The window opens small so it can sit beside your work. Press Command-F for full screen.

FREE
The whole game is free. No ads, no purchases, nothing to unlock, no account.

It's the same game on Mac, iPhone and iPad, and one download covers all of them.

oskiewar is made by Aesthetic Computer. It keeps changing — if something looks different next week, that's why.
