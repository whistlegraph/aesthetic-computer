oskiewar is a one-on-one stick-figure fight in a small gray dojo.

Punch, kick, dash, crouch and jump. Grab somebody and throw them. Raise a bubble to shield yourself. Weapons turn up on the ledges, so pick one up and use it. Limbs come off. Rounds are short and the score carries from one to the next.

PLAYING WITH PEOPLE
Every fight has a room, and the room is a web address. Send it to a friend and they open it in a web browser, with no install and no account, and take the second chair. Anyone else who opens the address watches. While you wait, a training dummy stands in the other corner for you to practise on.

CONTROLS
Keyboard: WASD or the arrow keys move. Q and E punch, Q and E together grab, Shift kicks, Space jumps, Shift+Q raises the bubble.
Two players on one keyboard: the second player uses I J K L to move, with U, O, ; and ' as the buttons. Press one of their keys and the fight becomes player one against player two.
Controller: the stick moves, X and Y punch, A kicks, B jumps. Two controllers give two players.
The window opens small so it can sit beside your work. Press Command-F for full screen.

FREE
The whole game is free. There are no ads and no purchases, nothing to unlock, and no account needed to play.

It's the same game on Mac, iPhone and iPad, and one download covers all of them. It also plays in any browser at oskiewar.com.

oskiewar is made by Aesthetic Computer. It keeps changing, so if something looks different next week, that's why.
