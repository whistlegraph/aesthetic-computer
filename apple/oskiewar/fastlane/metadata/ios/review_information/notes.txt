The app has no accounts and no sign-in, so no demo login is provided.

How to play: the app opens on the title screen. Tap "start" and your fighter walks in through the front doors into the park. The d-pad on the left moves. The four buttons on the right are kick (A), punch (B), shield bubble (X) and use item (Y). Walk into a skateboard, kart, chalk or weapon to pick it up. Drag an empty part of the screen to turn the camera; pinch to zoom.

Multiplayer: every room has an address at oskiewar.com/<room name>, and the room name is shown on screen. Opening that address in a web browser on a second device joins the same room. In a fight, the first visitor takes the second chair and later visitors watch.


How the app is built: the game engine is JavaScript. On iPhone and iPad it renders in WKWebView, loading the current build from oskiewar.com; if the network is unavailable it falls back to a copy of the game bundled in the app. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no third-party analytics SDKs.

Content: stylized stick-figure fighting with cartoon weapons (guns, grenades, axes). Hits leave cartoon blood marks and limbs can detach. There is no text chat.
