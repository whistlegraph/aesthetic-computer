Aesthetic.Computer is a computer you play like an instrument. There is one prompt. You type a word and it becomes something to use — a keyboard, a brush, a game, a room with people in it.

The words are called pieces. There are hundreds. You learn them the way you learn chords: a few at first, then by ear.

Some to start with...
 - `notepat` is a keyboard for ten fingers.
 - `line`, `shape`, `rect` and `oval` are brushes. `dl` saves the painting.
 - `juke` plays the pop catalog, songs made here.
 - `chat` is whoever else is around.
 - `baktok` teaches you to talk backward.
 - `camera` takes pictures.
 - `help` lists the rest.

Pieces are written in JavaScript or in KidLisp, a small language for moving pictures. Each one has a short name you can hand to someone else.

Sign in for a @handle. What you make stays with it.
