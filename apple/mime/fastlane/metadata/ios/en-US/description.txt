Every file has a MIME type: image/png, text/x-lisp, video/mp4, application/x-doom-wad. MIME.ac flips through what people make across Aesthetic Computer, KidLisp and Whistlegraph by type, one work per screen, like TV channels.

Swipe up through paintings, KidLisp programs, interactive pieces, Whistlegraphs, Doom levels, moods and saved Oskiewar rounds. Tap a piece to play with it. Tap its code to get a QR code for another phone.

Tap a type at the bottom right to make your own: a painting, a piece, a Doom level or a room. Talk or type what you want, and every version is kept. Tap a version number to remix someone's work into your own copy, with credit.

Watching needs no account. Making work uses an Aesthetic Computer sign-in.

Rules: mime.ac/terms. Tap anyone's @handle to report a card or hide their work. Every report is reviewed within 24 hours.
