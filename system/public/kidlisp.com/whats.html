What's KidLisp?

A tiny Lisp for pictures, motion and sound. One line is a program.

Write some Learn the words 
purple, ink, line, blur 5

The most played KidLisp piece. Four words.

How it reads

red

A color by itself fills the screen.

(wipe blue)
(ink yellow)
(circle 64 64 32)

Parentheses group a word with its arguments. Every line runs every frame.

(0.5s (wipe (? red yellow blue)))
(ink white)
(line)

A time prefix runs its body on a beat. A question mark picks at random. Bare words take sensible defaults.

($bop)
(zoom 0.97)
(spin 1)

Every saved piece gets a short code. Call it by name inside another piece.

Where it runs

aesthetic.computer

Type a line at the prompt, or open a saved piece by its code. The interpreter is one JavaScript file.

kidlisp.com

The editor. Write, run, publish and browse pieces in the browser.

AC Native OS

Laptops that boot straight into Aesthetic Computer. The same interpreter runs under QuickJS on bare metal.

iPhone and iPad

The Aesthetic Computer, MIME and Whistlegraph apps run pieces in a web view. MIME is a feed you swipe through.

Xbox

Pieces hot-deploy to the console inside the oskiewar shell. The game's props and vehicles are written in a KidLisp dialect that compiles into it.

Tezos

Keeps are pieces minted as tokens. Each one is a self-contained bundle of the piece and its runtime on IPFS.

Menu Band

The Mac menu bar instrument shows pieces in a floating window, and carries a Swift port of the core words.

Aesel

The terminal harness writes .lisp pieces with an agent, pushes them to your phone and publishes them under your handle.

Frames and Ableton

FF1 displays show a piece full screen. A Max for Live device puts one in a Live set.

Game Boy, Playdate, WASM

Compilers turn small pieces into handheld ROMs and a standalone rasterizer.

Papers

The language, its 118 built-ins, card-sized programs, and where it should live, written up as papers.
