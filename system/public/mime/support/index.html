MIME.ac Support

Questions, problems, or something you saw in the feed: write to mail@aesthetic.computer. We answer within a few days, and reports of objectionable work within 24 hours.

In the app

  • Swipe up and down to move through the feed.
  • Tap a piece to play with it; tap its code (top left) to go back to scrolling.
  • Tap a card’s code to see its QR code and share it.
  • Tap someone’s @handle to report that card or hide their work.
  • Tap a MIME type (bottom right) to make your own work of that type.
