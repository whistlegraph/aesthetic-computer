MIME.ac Rules

MIME.ac shows work people make across Aesthetic Computer, KidLisp and Whistlegraph. By posting work or using the app you agree to these rules. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive people.

What you post

  • No harassment, hate, threats or bullying.
  • Nothing sexual, graphically violent or illegal, and no spam or scams.
  • Post only what is yours to share.

Reporting and hiding

Tap anyone’s @handle on a card to report that card or to hide everything they make. Hiding takes effect on your phone at once. Every report is reviewed within 24 hours: work that breaks these rules is removed, and people who break them lose their place in the feed.

Your account

Making work needs an Aesthetic Computer account. You can delete it from the app (Account › Delete AC account) or by writing to mail@aesthetic.computer. How data is handled: privacy policy.
