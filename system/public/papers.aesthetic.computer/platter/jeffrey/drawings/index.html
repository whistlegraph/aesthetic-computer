History, not portfolio

The archive begins with drawings completed in 2006 and runs through August 2016. It holds sketchbooks, digital experiments, figures, flowers, portraits, collaborations, teaching contexts, and named working groups—not only selected finished works.

Jeffrey built the Rails catalog in 2015 in the spirit of Dieter Maurer's Early Pictures and Enrique Mallen's On-line Picasso Project: a picture practice documented through when, where, how, and with whom its images came into being.
