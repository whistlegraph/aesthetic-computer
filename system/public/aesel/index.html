In Aesthetic Computer

at the prompt

aesthetic.computer

Type a word the prompt doesn’t know and Aesel sketches it in KidLisp, with a tune, then runs it under a new $code.

under your @handle

aesthetic.computer/@you/name

Signed in, every save publishes the piece. A QR code opens it on any phone, live.

in KidLisp

kidlisp.com

Aesel writes .lisp pieces. Each one gets a $code and opens in KidLisp Lab.

as a brush

nopaint.art

Describe a brush and paint with it. The painting, undo and zoom come with it.

in the feed

mime.ac

Pictures, sound, video and ROMs up to 8 MiB post under your @handle.

as a paper

papers.aesthetic.computer

The same conversation writes an archival LaTeX paper with its source inside.
