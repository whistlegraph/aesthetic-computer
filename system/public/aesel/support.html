Getting started

Open Aesel, review the sharing notice, and sign into Aesthetic Computer. Claim a handle if you do not have one. Choose a media type, describe what you want to make, and refine it through conversation.

Hosted AI works without installing Claude or Codex. An internet connection is required, and generation limits apply.
