§Cover Letterv0 · 2026-11-06 · Jeffrey edits before filing

To the Ignite Reading team,

Your posting for a Senior Engineering Manager, Infrastructure & Data Engineering, is what brought me to the careers page. I read it twice. It is a job for someone who has run two teams and a SOC2 audit, and I have done neither. I am writing anyway, through the general application, because the mission is one I have been circling for years, and I think there is a product or learning-engineering seat where I would do my best work.

Instruments.

I build instruments that teach by being played. Aesthetic Computer is a creative-computing runtime I started in 2021 and have maintained alone since: a browser where you type a word and a program loads. Its language, KidLisp, fits a program in a text message. People have saved 18,650 of them under 3,069 handles. Two weeks ago I shipped Typist (oskiewar.com/typist), a typing game in which a child walks a hall of doors, and each door opens only when they type the word on it. Even halls use short real words. Odd halls use consonant-vowel nonsense syllables, because a nonsense word cannot be guessed, so it tests decoding and nothing else. Each correct letter sounds a note. Each finished syllable is spoken back in a recorded voice the moment its vowel lands, so the child hears what they just built.

Kids first.

Before any of this I ran children's activities at a bookstore in Naples, Florida, and coached boys' gymnastics for two years. Teaching has run alongside the engineering ever since: Parsons, UCLA, Southern Oregon University, and later this month a workshop at UCLA Design Media Arts on building coding harnesses.

The stack.

The engineering is real. One person runs the whole stack: a Linux build I ship to surplus laptops, a realtime session server, the web monolith, a fleet of 31 MCP servers, and a terminal harness I wrote so Claude could work inside the platform under human approval. The section of your posting on AI-assisted engineering describes my daily practice. The repository is public and holds 25,000 commits. What I lack is the management résumé. What I have is fourteen years of shipping software that strangers and students learn by touching.

If a role opens where someone needs to build the thing a six-year-old sees, or the tool a tutor holds during a fifteen-minute lesson, I would like to be on your list.

— Jeffrey Alan Scudder · @jeffrey · Los Angeles, CA
