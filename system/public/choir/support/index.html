Support

Write to mail@aesthetic.computer. A person reads every message, and we answer within two days.

Signing in

Enter your email and type the six-digit code we send. No password. If the code does not arrive, check spam and wait a minute before asking for another.

Joining

choir is invite only. A member can send you an invite link; open it on your iPhone. Each member has five invites.

Someone is bothering you

Long-press their message. Hide removes everything they post from your view. Report sends the message to us; we act on reports within 24 hours. For anything urgent, email us with "urgent" in the subject. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number.

Notifications

Chat and Discover notifications switch on and off separately in your account settings.

Deleting a post or your account

Long-press your own message to delete it. To delete your account, open your account and tap Delete account; it is erased after 14 days. Details are on the privacy page.
