Terms and community rules

Updated October 12, 2026

choir is an invite-only room for artists to talk about art and culture. By joining you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use choir.

The rules

choir has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Do not post:

  • harassment, bullying, threats, or slurs, or anything telling someone to hurt themselves;
  • hate against people for their race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or origin;
  • sexual content involving minors, of any kind, ever;
  • pornography, or intimate images of anyone without their consent;
  • violent or gory content meant to shock;
  • other people's private information, such as addresses, phone numbers or private messages;
  • spam, scams, or impersonation;
  • anything illegal, or work you do not have the right to share.

Art that deals with hard subjects is welcome when it is shared to talk about it. Say what it is.

How choir enforces them

  • A filter refuses messages that contain slurs or self-harm taunts.
  • Long-press any message to Report it or to Hide its author. Hiding takes their messages out of your view right away.
  • We review every report within 24 hours. Content that breaks the rules is removed, and the person who posted it loses access to choir.
  • We may remove anything, or close any account, that puts members at risk, with or without a report.

Your account

You must be 18 or older. Keep your sign-in email to yourself; you are responsible for what is posted from your account. Each member can invite a few people and is asked to invite only people they know.

Your work

You keep the rights to what you post. You let choir store it and show it to members so the room works, until you delete it. Do not repost another member's work outside choir without asking them.

The service

choir is new and offered as it is. It may change, pause, or lose data, and we are not liable for what members post. Discover links to other websites we do not control.

Changes and contact

If these terms change in a way that matters, the app will ask you to agree again. Reports, questions and appeals go to mail@aesthetic.computer. See also the privacy page.
