# No Paint

> Paint with AI image models or local brushes using your Aesthetic Computer account.

The website shares its cloud model catalog, Braincell prices and high-level UI definitions with the No Paint desktop app. Local Stable Diffusion, fleet models and Real-ESRGAN are available through an explicitly paired desktop companion. Browser paintings and history are kept locally. Done publishes the accepted painting and steps under the signed-in AC handle.
