# Note Paint

> Paint with a note and a brain: write what should change, press Paint, keep it or say No.

The AI painting studio of No Paint, at its own address. Cloud brains require an Aesthetic Computer account and Braincells. A painting's address is notepaint.art/#code.

## Resources

- [Studio](https://notepaint.art/): Public entry point.
- [No Paint 2021](https://nopaint.art/): The original Construct build, preserved.
- [Gallery](https://nopaint.art/gallery/): Public painting records.
