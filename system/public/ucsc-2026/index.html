Preliminary screen is on the Teaching Statement alone
Every student piece is a URL. Every class session is a room people enter from their phones. The instrument is small enough to learn in an hour and open enough to read all the way down.

That is the one thing this application argues. Aesthetic Computer was built to be an online classroom before the word "online" was a requirement, and the Creative Technologies major, a two-year, transfer-friendly, camera-on program at a Hispanic-Serving Institution, is the student body it was built for.

§What they ask for, and the answerfrom the posting

Creative coding environmentsProcessing, p5.js, openFrameworks — or "other open-source and/or custom applications." Aesthetic Computer and KidLisp are the custom application; p5.js is taught alongside so students see the same ideas twice.
Real-time multimediaMax, Pd, SuperCollider, Orca. The AC runtime is a live-coded, hot-reloading, multiplayer real-time system; Menu Band is a native synth and MIDI instrument.
Sound and musicnotepat (a chromatic phone keyboard), Menu Band, and Note(s)pat(ial), a spatial piece for six laptops premiered at CultureHub LA in September 2026 and proposed to ICLC 2027.
Online pedagogyUCLA DMA's Interactivity summer section (2024) taught on AC across time zones; synchronous live coding on a shared canvas, asynchronous reading and code review.
FLOSS and critical technologyThe whole platform is public source. Its papers press documents it. Its use of language models is bounded, logged, and written up rather than assumed.
A just scholarly communityMaterials are free and forkable, run on the cheapest phone in the class, and the first success comes in the first hour. Student work leaves with the student, at a public address they own.

§The two syllabioutlines · PDFs to follow

Introduction to Creative Coding: The Room Is a URLTen weeks, no prerequisites, no purchases. Copy → change → make → show. Weeks 1–6 on Aesthetic Computer, weeks 7–8 rebuilding a piece in p5.js, week 10 a public showing on phones that aren't the author's. Signature assignment Borrow: make a third piece that needs a classmate's and yours to exist, credited in the first line.
Instruments You Can Send: Sound, Code, and ListeningTen weeks from notepat to a networked concert. Tone, tuning, time, samples, a listening seminar, two students on one clock, six laptops in six rooms. Signature assignment A scale of your own: a playable non-equal-tempered tuning at a URL, one minute recorded, 300 words on where the ratios came from and what it is bad at.
