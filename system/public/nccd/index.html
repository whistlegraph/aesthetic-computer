NCCd is a Mac app for the NELA Computer Club. It shows the club's Signal chats and circles and the club wiki in one window. The d is for donut.

How it works

  1. Open it. If your Mac has Homebrew, NCCd installs its Signal engine (signal-cli) itself.
  2. Signal Link Signal to chat: announcements, chat, and circles. Scan a QR code with your phone, as you do for Signal Desktop.
  3. NCC Sign into NCC for wiki & code. The club login shows inside the app.

The two logins are separate. Neither is required.

Good to know

  • Messages stay end-to-end encrypted. NCCd is a linked device, signal-cli does the encryption, and no Aesthetic Computer server is in the path.
  • NCCd updates itself. It verifies the hash and the signature of each update.
  • The app is signed and notarized by Apple. It has no analytics. The source is public.
  • The download link counts downloads. It keeps your address only as a keyed hash.
  • To remove NCCd, unlink it in Signal on your phone and delete the app.
