Aesthetic Computer's Privacy Policy

Aesthetic Computer keeps your paintings and artworks on a server so they can be shared and viewed.

You can associate an email with a @handle to link with your artwork and activity.

We may request camera access for camera and handtracking, and microphone access for recording.

Some features use AI and voice services. Multiplayer games connect to realtime servers where your presence and chat are visible to others.

We use cookies and third-party services for login, analytics, and payments.

We federate handles with Sotce Net — same email means shared @handle.

User media is federated through at.aesthetic.computer, our ATProto server.

We do not sell your data.

Deleting your account

Enter delete-erase-and-forget-me, or use Delete account in the settings of the Aesel app. Before you confirm, it shows what will be deleted, what will stay, and any braincells you would lose. The web page also lets you download a copy of your data first.

Your account locks right away and is deleted 14 days later. We email you a link to keep it until then, and another email when it is gone.

We delete your @handle, paintings, pieces, moods, tapes, clocks, news posts, chat messages, mail, uploaded files, saved device keys, and the account itself, including its ATProto account at at.aesthetic.computer. Monthly gifts made with the same verified email stop.

Some things stay, without your name. KidLisp that is minted on Tezos is permanent on the blockchain and IPFS. KidLisp used in other people's pieces stays so their work keeps running. Purchase records stay as long as tax law requires. Usage logs keep their times and pages, but not who you were.

Your @handle cannot be claimed by anyone else for 90 days. If a Sotce Net account shares your email and handle, that account keeps the handle.

Unused braincells are lost. Refunds for App Store purchases go through Apple.

We stop hosting the IPFS files we pinned for KidLisp that was never minted, but other IPFS nodes may keep copies. Other ATProto and Bluesky services may keep copies of posts for a while after we delete them. Copies in our database backups disappear as those backups expire.

For questions, enter help.
