# Aesel donkey

The Aesel donkey wears small red reading glasses. This is a defining character feature for future 2D and 3D depictions, established September 25, 2026.

Bright-red frames sit low on the cream muzzle, leaving the expressive dark eyes visible above the rims. Use clear lenses, a plausible bridge, and slender temples supported near the ear bases. Preserve the oversized upright ears, blue-gray pencil fur, cream belly, short dark mane, dark hooves, tufted tail, and friendly natural donkey proportions seen in `../shared/assets/donkey-pencil-run-v2.png`.

For 3D, prefer glasses as a separate accessory attached to the head rig. Generated segmentation must be checked before claiming the glasses are independently editable. Existing run sprites predate the glasses; do not mistake their omission for the current character brief.
