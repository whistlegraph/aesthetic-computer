My first teaching experiences were at SVA in New York and RISD in Providence during the early 80s. In both cases I was an adjunct, showing up for my allotted three hours and then leaving. Later in the decade I was invited out to CalArts in Los Angeles, for a semester, and found an entirely different situation, one in which I was included in many meetings and discussions about the curriculum. A second semester there a year later confirmed a growing idea that art school could be a generative locus for continued research into the nature of art and its place in society, and in a surprising development I soon found myself dean of the Art School and responsible for giving concrete shape to my developing ideas.
