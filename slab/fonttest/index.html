Tiny text test

Sit the way you normally do at your terminal and keep that distance. Do not lean in.

Four characters flash for half a second between the two tick marks. Type what you saw, then press Return. Capitals matter. Guess when unsure. The text gets smaller when you are right and bigger when you are wrong, so about half of them should feel too small. That is the test working.

Press Return on an empty box to see a flash again once.
