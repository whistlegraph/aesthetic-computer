Your Mac keyboard is now three instruments in one. A new Keymap dropdown switches among Menu Band, AWSED, and MilkyTracker layouts. AWSED and MilkyTracker retain the real staggered positions of your physical keys, with unused caps left invisibly in place. MilkyTracker adds classic two-octave tracker keyjazz across ZSXDCVGBHNJM and Q2W3ER5T6Y7U while keeping 0 available for MIDI mode. Chord modifiers work in every layout, so you can explore major, minor, suspended, augmented, diminished, and fifth-rich harmony from any root.
